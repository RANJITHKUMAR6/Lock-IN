Cancellation, Refund and Delivery Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Grove is operated by Ranjith Kumar S, India.

Delivery

Grove Plus is a digital product. It is activated on your account as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, usually within a minute. If it is not active after one hour, email you@example.com with your payment reference.

Cancellation

Plus never renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. It simply ends when the period you bought runs out.

Refunds

You can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if you were charged twice, if payment succeeded but Plus was not activated, or if a technical problem stopped you using Plus features and we could not fix it. We do not refund after 7 days or for a change of mind once Plus has been used.

How to ask

Email you@example.com from your account email with your payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
