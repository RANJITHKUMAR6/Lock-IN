Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Grove is operated by Ranjith Kumar S, India.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, username and a one-way hash of your password (we cannot read your password). When you buy Grove Plus we store the order reference, plan, amount and status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay and never reach our servers.

Your progress

Tasks, points, pet and settings are stored in your browser on your own device. If you press "Back up to cloud", a copy is saved on our database so you can restore it later. We do not read it for any other purpose.

Cookies and third parties

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We use no advertising or analytics trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and payments load Razorpay Checkout, so those providers may see your IP address. Password-reset emails are sent through Resend.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. Email you@example.com to delete your account, cloud backup and personal data. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to access, correct or erase your data. Write to you@example.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic uses HTTPS, and login sessions use secure cookies. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
